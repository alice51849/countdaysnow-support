Step by step: set up a countdown widget

  1. Create an event with its date, and put it in a category if you like.
  2. Set repeating events, including an optional end date, and schedule reminders.
  3. Add a Home Screen widget in any size, or the Next 3 widget to see your three closest dates at once.
  4. Keep a live countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  5. Optionally show a second calendar next to the Gregorian date, such as Chinese lunar, Japanese, Hebrew, Persian, Indian, Islamic or Buddhist.
  6. Import only the Calendar events you choose, and archive milestones later without losing their history.

Plan backwards with Countdown Plan

Big date coming and prep slipping? Countdown Plan gives any event its own preparation steps. Each step is due a set number of days before its event, for example renew the passport 30 days before a trip or order the cake 7 days before a birthday. Change the event date and every step moves with it, and a repeating event starts each new cycle with a fresh plan. You see what is late, due today, due in the next 7 days and done across all your events.

Template for a trip:

  • 30 days before: renew the passport
  • 14 days before: confirm bookings
  • 7 days before: arrange pet care
  • 1 day before: pack and charge devices

Anniversary countdown and sharing

For anniversaries, set the event to repeat and let the milestone alerts handle the rest: day 100 and every anniversary get a gentle nudge. Choose from 100 countdown styles, each with its own palette and layout, and export a share card as a square, portrait or story image. A single countdown can be shared with other CountDaysNow users and updated together.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see how many days until an event?

Create the event and check the Ahead view, or add it to a Home Screen widget.

Which widgets are available?

Home Screen widgets in every size including iPad Extra Large, the Next 3 widget, a Lock Screen countdown and a live countdown in the Dynamic Island.

Can it count days since a date?

Yes. The Since view shows how long ago past events happened.

Does it remind me before anniversaries?

Yes: 7, 3 and 1 day before, on the day, at day 100 and on every anniversary.

What is Countdown Plan?

Preparation steps for an event, each due a set number of days before it, which move when the event date changes.

Is it a subscription?

No. One purchase unlocks everything for good, with no ads and no account.
