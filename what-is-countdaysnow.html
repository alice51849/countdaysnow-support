What is CountDaysNow?

CountDaysNow is an event counter for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It keeps future, current and past events in three views (Ahead, Today and Since) with precise day counts and a date calculator. Countdown Plan lets you plan backwards from a date: each event gets its own preparation steps due a set number of days before it, and the steps move when the date changes.

Who is it for?

Anyone counting down to trips, birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, exams or deadlines, or counting days since a milestone. It also suits people who track dates on another calendar, such as the Chinese lunar, Japanese, Hebrew, Persian, Indian, Islamic or Buddhist calendar, and families or friends who want to share one countdown.

How does pricing work?

According to the App Store description, the free edition supports up to 3 active events, 3 categories, 1 reminder per event and one selected event widget, and Countdown Plan is free. A single Lifetime Pro purchase, never a subscription, unlocks unlimited events and categories, all 100 skins, advanced recurrence, reminders and calendars, every widget, the full Apple Watch experience, photo backgrounds, Face ID, iCloud and JSON backup and high-resolution sharing. There are no ads.

How is it different?

  • Countdown Plan: preparation steps tied to each event, with late, due today, next 7 days and done across all events.
  • Live countdowns on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, widgets in every size and a Next 3 widget.
  • Milestone alerts 7, 3 and 1 day before, on the day, at day 100 and on every anniversary.
  • A second calendar beside the Gregorian date, from 15 calendars.
  • No accounts, advertising, third-party analytics or tracking, and a local snapshot before every update.

Want to try it on your iPhone? CountDaysNow counts down to the dates that matter and helps you get ready for them.

More questions

Does it sync between iPhone and iPad?

Yes, with Lifetime Pro, through your private iCloud.

Can I share a countdown?

Yes. With Lifetime Pro a single countdown can be shared with other CountDaysNow users and updated together; reminders and other events stay private.

Can I see the skins before buying?

Yes. You can browse all 100 countdown styles before unlocking them.

CountDaysNow sends no data to developer servers.
